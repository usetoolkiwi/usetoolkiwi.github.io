Percentage Calculator

Three calculators in one: percent of a number, what percent, and percentage change.

How to use the Percentage Calculator

  1. Use the first row to find a percentage of a number (e.g. a discount).
  2. Use the second row to find what percent one number is of another (e.g. a test score).
  3. Use the third row for percentage increase or decrease (e.g. a price change).

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Divide the percentage by 100 and multiply by the number. 15% of 200 = 0.15 × 200 = 30.

How do I calculate percentage change?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, then multiply by 100.

How do I work out a discount?

Use the first row: enter the discount percent and the original price. Subtract the answer from the price to get the sale price.
