Frequently asked questions

How do I convert WebP to JPG?

Choose JPG as the output format, then drop your WebP files onto the box. Each one is converted instantly.

What happens to transparent backgrounds when converting to JPG?

JPG does not support transparency, so transparent areas become white. Choose PNG or WebP to keep transparency.

Which format should I use?

JPG for photos, PNG for graphics and screenshots with sharp edges or transparency, WebP for the smallest files on websites.
