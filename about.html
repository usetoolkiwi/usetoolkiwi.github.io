About ToolKiwi

ToolKiwi is a collection of simple, free tools for everyday jobs: shrinking photos, combining PDFs, making QR codes, counting words and doing quick calculations.

Every tool runs inside your web browser. When you compress an image or merge a PDF, the work happens on your own device. Your files are never uploaded to our servers, which makes the tools fast and keeps your documents private.

The site is free because it shows a small number of ads. We add new tools regularly — if there is one you would like, please tell us.
