Password Generator

Create strong, random passwords you can trust. Generated on your device with cryptographic randomness.

How to use the Password Generator

  1. Choose a length — 16 or more characters is recommended.
  2. Tick the character types you want.
  3. Click Copy password and save it in your password manager.

Frequently asked questions

Are these passwords really random?

Yes. They use crypto.getRandomValues, the same secure random source browsers use for encryption.

Do you store or see my passwords?

No. Passwords are created on your device and never sent anywhere.

How long should a password be?

At least 12 characters, ideally 16 or more, with a mix of character types. Use a different password for every account.
