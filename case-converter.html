Change text to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case and more with one click.

How to use the Case Converter

  1. Paste your text.
  2. Click the case you want.
  3. Click Copy result.

Frequently asked questions

What is Title Case?

Every major word starts with a capital letter, while short words like 'and', 'of' and 'the' stay lowercase unless they start the title.

What is Sentence case?

Only the first letter of each sentence is capitalised, like normal writing.

I accidentally typed with Caps Lock on — can this fix it?

Yes. Paste the text and click Sentence case or lowercase.
