Loan EMI Calculator

Find the monthly payment for a home, car or personal loan, with total interest and a yearly schedule.

How to use the Loan EMI Calculator

  1. Enter the loan amount in any currency.
  2. Enter the yearly interest rate.
  3. Enter the tenure in years or months.
  4. Read your monthly EMI, total interest and repayment schedule.

Frequently asked questions

What is an EMI?

An Equated Monthly Instalment — the fixed amount you pay every month until the loan is repaid. It covers part interest and part principal.

What formula is used?

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of months.

Does this include fees or insurance?

No. It covers principal and interest only. Ask your lender about processing fees and insurance.
