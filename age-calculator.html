Age Calculator

Work out an exact age in years, months and days — and how long until the next birthday.

How to use the Age Calculator

  1. Enter the date of birth.
  2. Leave the second date as today, or change it to find the age on any date.
  3. Your exact age appears instantly.

Frequently asked questions

How is age calculated?

Full years are counted first, then remaining whole months, then remaining days — the same way age is counted on official forms.

Can I calculate age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the 'Age on this date' field to any date.

What if I was born on 29 February?

In non-leap years, the next birthday is counted as 1 March.
