How to use the Image Resizer

  1. Choose or drop an image.
  2. Type a new width or height — the other side updates automatically when the aspect ratio is locked.
  3. Or pick a quick scale such as 50%.
  4. Click Resize & download.

Frequently asked questions

What size should an image be for social media?

Common sizes: Instagram post 1080 × 1080, Instagram story 1080 × 1920, YouTube thumbnail 1280 × 720, Facebook cover 1640 × 624.

Will enlarging an image make it sharper?

No. Making an image bigger than the original spreads the same detail over more pixels, so it can look softer.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Resizing happens entirely in your browser.
