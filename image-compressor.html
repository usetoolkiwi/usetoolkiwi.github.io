How to use the Image Compressor

  1. Drop one or more images onto the box, or click it to choose files.
  2. Move the quality slider — 70% is a good balance of size and sharpness.
  3. Choose WebP if you want the smallest possible file.
  4. Click Download next to each image, or Download all.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to compress private photos here?

Yes. Compression happens inside your browser using your own device. Your images are never uploaded to any server.

Why is my PNG not getting much smaller?

PNG is a lossless format, so quality settings have little effect. Choose JPG or WebP as the output format for a much smaller file.

How many images can I compress at once?

There is no fixed limit. Very large batches depend on your device's memory.

Does compression reduce image dimensions?

No. Width and height stay the same. To change dimensions, use the Image Resizer.
