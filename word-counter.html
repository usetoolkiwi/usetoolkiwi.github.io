Word Counter

Count words, characters, sentences and reading time instantly as you type or paste.

How to use the Word Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the box.
  2. The counts update live as you write.
  3. Check the top keywords to see which words you repeat most.

Frequently asked questions

How is reading time calculated?

Using an average silent reading speed of 238 words per minute. Speaking time uses 140 words per minute.

Does it count characters with spaces?

Yes — both with and without spaces are shown.

Is my text saved anywhere?

No. Everything stays in your browser and disappears when you close the page.
