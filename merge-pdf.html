Merge PDF

Combine several PDF files into a single PDF. Drag to add, reorder with the arrows, then merge.

Drop PDF files here or click to chooseAdd two or more PDFs

    How to use the Merge PDF

    1. Add two or more PDF files.
    2. Use the ↑ and ↓ arrows to put them in the right order.
    3. Click Merge PDFs — the combined file downloads straight away.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is there a file size limit?

    No fixed limit. Very large files depend on your device's memory.

    Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

    No. Merging happens entirely on your device, which makes it safe for contracts and personal documents.

    Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

    Not yet. Remove the password first, then merge.
